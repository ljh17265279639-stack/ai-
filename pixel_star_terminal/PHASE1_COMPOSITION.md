# Phase 1 composition · Saturn hero

- Saturn is the single dominant background subject.
- Full globe and full rings must remain visible; no ring-edge crop.
- Desktop: broad ring ellipse with Saturn filling most of the useful background while retaining black negative space.
- Mobile: farther camera preserves the complete ring silhouette in portrait orientation.
- Space remains nearly pure black with sparse fixed stars; no nebula wallpaper.
- Saturn uses realistic oblateness, 26.73° axial tilt and a low-fill directional lighting setup.
