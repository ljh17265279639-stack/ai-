# PIXEL STAR · Phase 1.4

## Scope
- Deep Space Environment enhancement
- Stronger Saturn ring presence

## What changed
- Added a very faint procedural Milky Way style dust band made from real-time 3D particles (no image skybox).
- Added two distant dark star clusters to break the monotony of a plain black starfield.
- Added a sparse foreground dust layer for extra depth.
- Added a rare low-frequency meteor / distant streak event.
- Increased Saturn ring presence with a brighter main color pass, deeper under-layer, additive highlight pass, and thicker edge rims.
- Increased subtle ring particle visibility while keeping the approved Saturn composition and camera framing.

## Preserved
- Approved Phase 1.2 reference-locked Saturn composition.
- Phase 1.3 slow Saturn rotation and dynamic deep space motion controls.
- Reduced-motion compatibility.
