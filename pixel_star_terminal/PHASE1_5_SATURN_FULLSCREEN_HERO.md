# PIXEL STAR · Phase 1.5

## Saturn Full-Screen Hero
This version shifts the background strategy away from subtle left-side deep-space details and toward a stronger full-screen Saturn hero composition.

### Changes
- Reduced the apparent viewing tilt of Saturn's rings by lowering the camera's vertical viewing angle.
- Enlarged Saturn and the ring system so the planet occupies more of the screen.
- Tightened the camera framing for a more immersive, full-screen hero feel.
- Slightly re-centered the composition for stronger page-wide visual presence.
- Preserved the approved ring thickness and slow Saturn rotation.
